# Case study text: keep a blank line between paragraphs
challenge: |
  How do you help a busy community center turn first-time visitors into donors, volunteers and neighbors who show up?

  Common Ground is a neighborhood community center that runs a food pantry, after-school programs, senior services and weekly community dinners. Like many small nonprofits, it relies on word of mouth, flyers and a Facebook page, and its online presence didn't reflect the warmth of the place or the scale of its impact. People who wanted to help often didn't know how to donate, where to sign up to volunteer, or what was happening this week. The challenge was to create a website concept that felt as welcoming as walking through the center's front door, while making the most important actions (giving, volunteering and attending events) clear and easy for everyone.
solution: |
  A responsive, accessible website concept was designed to give Common Ground a home online that matches its mission.

  Using a deep forest-green, warm cream and sunflower-yellow palette, friendly serif headings and a custom illustration of the neighborhood, we created a look that feels hopeful, local and human rather than corporate.

  The homepage is built around action. A Donate button stays visible in the header, the hero offers two clear paths, "Donate now" and "Volunteer with us," and a top announcement bar highlights timely campaigns like the Winter Coat Drive. An impact section shares the center's story in numbers (meals served, active volunteers, free programs and families supported), so visitors can see what their support makes possible. Program pages for the Food Pantry, Youth Programs and Senior Services explain who each program is for and how to get help, and an events section keeps recurring gatherings like Thursday community dinners easy to find.

  Accessibility was a priority from the start: high-contrast colors, readable type sizes, large tap targets on mobile and simple navigation, so neighbors of every age and ability can find what they need.
results: |
  The result is a cohesive, welcoming website concept that brings Common Ground's programs, events and ways to help together in one place, balancing an inviting look with clear, practical calls to action.

  The finished project demonstrates a complete approach to nonprofit website design, from shaping the story and organizing content around donors, volunteers and the people the center serves, to accessible, mobile-first design.

  Created as a portfolio and pitch concept for a fictional community center, the website shows how a small nonprofit can move beyond flyers and social media to an online presence that builds trust, inspires giving and connects neighbors with the help they need.
