# Case study text: keep a blank line between paragraphs
challenge: |
  How do you turn a salon that lives entirely on social media into a place clients can find, explore and book in just a few taps?

  Autumn's Nail Spa has a loyal local following and creative nail work that shines on Instagram, but it had no website of its own. Services, hours and contact details were spread across Instagram, Facebook and Yelp, and booking meant calling or sending a message. The challenge was to create a website concept that captured the salon's style while making it simple for new and returning clients to discover services, get inspired by past work and book their next appointment.
solution: |
  A mobile-first, responsive website concept was designed to give Autumn's Nail Spa a polished home online.

  Using a warm ivory and deep cherry-red palette, elegant serif typography and bright imagery of the salon's work, we created a digital space that feels as relaxing and refined as a visit to the spa itself.

  The experience includes a clear services menu covering everything from classic and gel manicures to spa pedicures, acrylics, dip powder, custom nail art and waxing, along with a gallery of the salon's designs and accessible location, hours and contact information. At the center is online booking, where clients choose a service, pick their preferred nail technician or "any available," and reserve a time that works for them, any time of day. Clear navigation and a prominent Book Now button guide visitors from browsing designs to booking their appointment.
results: |
  The result is a cohesive, easy-to-navigate website concept that brings the salon's services, creative work and essential business information together in one place, balancing visual appeal with practical booking functionality.

  The finished project demonstrates a complete approach to building a booking website, from brand interpretation and content organization to mobile-first design and appointment scheduling setup.

  Created as a portfolio and pitch concept, the website serves as a tangible example of how a local salon can move beyond social media to a professional online presence that saves staff time and makes booking effortless for clients.
