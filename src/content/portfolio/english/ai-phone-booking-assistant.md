# Case study text: keep a blank line between paragraphs
challenge: |
  Many of Autumn's Nail Spa's clients still prefer to call. But while technicians are busy with customers, the phone often goes unanswered, and every booking taken by phone has to be scheduled by hand. Missed calls mean missed appointments, especially after hours.
solution: |
  Alongside the new website's online booking, we set up Wix's AI Phone Agent on the salon's phone line. It answers every call, day or night, in a friendly voice, using the salon's website content to answer questions about services, prices, hours and availability. When a caller wants an appointment, the agent texts them a direct link to the salon's booking page, where they pick a service, a technician or "any available," and a time. Wix Bookings confirms the appointment and sends reminders automatically.

  Every call is summarized in the dashboard, and calls that need a personal follow-up are flagged and sent to the owner by email or push notification.
results: |
  Clients can still book the way they always have, by phone, or online, and no one has to stop working to answer calls or schedule appointments by hand. The salon never misses a booking opportunity, even after hours.

  Created as a portfolio and pitch concept, it shows how a small salon can modernize booking without changing what its clients are used to.
