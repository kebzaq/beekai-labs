challenge: |
  How do you translate the warmth, personality, and community spirit of a neighborhood café into a digital experience?

  The Velvet Haus Cafe has a distinctive atmosphere rooted in comfort, culture, and connection. The challenge was to create a website concept that captured that identity while making it easy for customers to explore the menu, discover events, and plan their next visit.
solution: |
  A responsive, multi-page website concept was designed to bring The Velvet Haus experience online.

  Using a rich olive-green palette, warm imagery, inviting typography, and intentional layouts, I created a digital environment that reflects the café's character.

  The experience includes an organized menu with product imagery and descriptions, dedicated pages for community events and the café's story, and accessible location and contact information. Clear navigation and calls to action guide visitors toward exploring the menu, ordering, and visiting the café.
results: |
  The result is a cohesive, interactive website concept that captures the personality of The Velvet Haus while balancing visual appeal with practical functionality.

  The finished project demonstrates a complete approach to website creation, from brand interpretation and content development to responsive design and implementation.

  Created independently as a portfolio and pitch concept, the website serves as a tangible example of how thoughtful design can strengthen a local business's digital presence.
