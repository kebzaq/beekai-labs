# Case study text: keep a blank line between paragraphs
challenge: |
  What happens after a customer clicks "Request service"?

  For Northside Heating & Air, the answer was: not much, at least not right away. Website requests landed in a shared inbox, where they waited until someone in the office had time to check. Customers didn't know if their request went through, details were copied by hand into spreadsheets, and technicians often heard about urgent jobs late. After the work was done, no one had time to ask happy customers for a review. The challenge was to connect the website to the tools the team already uses, so every request is handled in seconds without adding more work.
solution: |
  We designed an automated workflow that starts the moment a customer submits the service request form.

  The customer instantly gets a text and email confirming their request, with what happens next and a number to call for emergencies. At the same time, the request is saved to the company's CRM with all the details (name, address, service type and urgency), and the team gets an alert on their phones, with urgent "no heat" requests flagged at the top. Once the job is marked complete, the customer automatically receives a thank-you message with a link to leave a Google review, followed by a reminder about seasonal maintenance a few months later.

  The workflow runs on reliable, affordable tools (website forms, Google Workspace, a CRM, SMS and an automation platform), so there's no custom software for the team to maintain.
results: |
  The result is a hands-off system where no request gets lost, customers hear back in seconds instead of hours, and the office spends its time scheduling jobs instead of copying data.

  Created as a portfolio and pitch concept, it shows how a small service business can turn its website into a lead machine that works around the clock.
