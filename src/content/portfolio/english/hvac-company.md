# Case study text: keep a blank line between paragraphs
challenge: |
  When a furnace breaks in the middle of a Central New York winter, how do you make sure the homeowner calls you first?

  Northside Heating & Air is a local heating and cooling company known for honest work, but its online presence wasn't keeping up. Customers search on their phones, often in a hurry, and calls were missed while technicians were out on jobs. The challenge was to create a website concept that builds trust in seconds and makes it easy to call, request service or book a visit, any time of day.
solution: |
  A fast, mobile-first website concept built to turn visitors into calls and service requests.

  A navy-and-white palette with a bold red accent for every call and booking button, clean typography and real photos of technicians at work give the site a dependable, no-nonsense look that feels local and trustworthy.

  The experience puts the most important actions within one tap. The phone number and a Call now button stay visible in the header, and the hero leads with "Fast, honest heating & AC repair," same-day service across Central NY, a Book a visit button and the company's Google rating. Service cards for Furnace Repair, AC Installation and Maintenance Plans explain what's offered at a glance, while a service area section, licensing and trust details and an emergency call-to-action help homeowners decide quickly.

  An AI receptionist answers calls and website questions when the team is busy or after hours, books appointments straight into the schedule and sends the office an instant notification for every new service request, so no lead slips through the cracks.
results: |
  The result is a clear, conversion-focused website concept that brings Northside's services, reviews and booking options together in one place, balancing a professional look with fast, practical ways to get help.

  The finished project demonstrates a complete approach to building a home services website, from brand interpretation and content organization to mobile-first design, online booking and AI-powered call handling.

  Created as a portfolio and pitch concept, the website serves as a tangible example of how a local HVAC company can capture more calls, respond faster and win customers' trust before the first visit.
